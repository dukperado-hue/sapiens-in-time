S9075	Glass 5 · Tea
S9076	about 1700–1900 CE · the British Empire
S9077	The Chinese had drunk tea for thousands of years, but in the 18th century tea shipped from China by the East India Company became the drink of every class in Britain. Paying for so much tea, Britain sold opium into China, leading to the Opium Wars, and in the American colonies a tax on tea became a spark of revolution.
