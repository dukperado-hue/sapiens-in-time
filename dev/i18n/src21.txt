S6944	Rajagaha sat in a ring of five hills, joined by a dry-stone wall some 40 km long whose remains can still be seen. King Bimbisara ruled here in the Buddha’s time; later Magadha’s capital moved to Pataliputra. Its hot springs have drawn bathers since then.
